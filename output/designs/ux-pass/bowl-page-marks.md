# Bowl page marks

Status: built. Scott signed off on October 1, 2026, after four rounds of
mockups (`/mnt/project-files/ux-pass/bowl-page-marks/` in the project files).

## What changed

- The bowl page's stat line and the television's readout count with the marks
  My Bowls and the bowl picker already use: the film strip beside the eligible
  pool, the people beside the bowl's member count. When filters leave someone
  with nothing in the draw, the people mark becomes the amber reached/total
  ratio it replaces. "Drawing from" stays in the accessible name only.
- The method slip the television shows beside the bowl name now also ends the
  stat line on phone and desktop, in the place the ⓘ held, and opens How this
  bowl picks.
- The slip's print changed. A music player's two buttons are read without a
  legend: shuffle is chance and repeat is everyone in turn. Title-first is a
  bare shuffle. Person-first is shuffle and rotation is repeat, each with a
  round person badge on the slip's corner, because both pick a person before a
  title.

## Decisions

- Shuffle means title-first, not person-first. Scott's call: a flat shuffle
  over every title is what the button means everywhere else.
- A person printed small on the slip did not read as a person at phone size.
  The badge sits outside the slip and is solid, like an avatar on a message.
- The people count is the bowl's members, so it matches the picker.
- The television keeps the slip beside the bowl name; it has no ⓘ to replace.
